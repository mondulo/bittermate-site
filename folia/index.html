Captura

Libros que viste, guardados para después.

Fotografiá la tapa de un libro en una librería o en un diario. Cada captura queda pendiente hasta que la muevas a la wishlist o la descartes. La tarjeta guarda la foto, la fecha y los datos del libro, y la ubicación de la captura abre el mapa para ir a comprarlo a esa librería. El botón Código escanea el QR de un libro que te comparte otro usuario de Folia y lo agrega a tu biblioteca.

Wishlist

Los libros que querés comprar.

La wishlist reúne los libros que pasás desde Captura y los que agregás a mano. Podés fijar los que más querés arriba, ordenarlos, cambiar la vista, buscar y compartir un libro con un código QR. Cada uno guarda la foto de la tapa, la fecha y una nota sobre por qué te interesó, y cuando lo conseguís lo movés a la biblioteca.

Libreta

Una libreta por cada libro.

Cada libro tiene su libreta, con dos pestañas. En Notas escribís lo que pensás del libro. En Contexto armás un diario de lo que pasaba en tu vida mientras lo leías: dónde estabas, qué música escuchabas, qué más leías y cómo te sentías.

Folia

All your books, including the ones you want to buy, on iPhone and iPad.

App Store
Reading room

What you’re reading, at hand.

The last seven days of reading with the streak, a shortcut to the calendar, a quote about books and the books in progress with their progress.

  • Last 7 days, with streak
  • Reading calendar
  • Quotes about books
  • Books in progress, with their progress
Calendar

The month’s reading, day by day.

Each day shows the covers of what was read and how many readings there were. The month shows how many reading days it has so far.

  • Covers of what was read each day
  • Number of readings per day
  • Reading days of the month
Capture

Books you saw, saved for later.

Photograph the cover of a book in a bookstore or in a newspaper. Each capture stays pending until you move it to the wishlist or discard it. The card keeps the photo, the date and the book’s details, and the capture’s location opens the map so you can go buy it at that bookstore. The Code button scans the QR code of a book another Folia user shares with you and adds it to your library.

Wishlist

The books you want to buy.

The wishlist gathers the books you pass over from Capture and the ones you add by hand. You can pin your favorites to the top, sort them, change the view, search and share a book with a QR code. Each one keeps the cover photo, the date and a note about why it interested you, and when you get it you move it to the library.

Appearance

Customize your library.

Automatic, light or dark mode, alternative icons, paper and paper textures, the shelf material and the library view.

  • Views: covers, spines or mural (the mural spreads the books across shelves by status)
  • Shelf wood: mahogany, walnut, oak, ebony, ash and slate
  • Icons: Original, Christmas, Snowy and Alternative
  • Papers: Cream, Pink, Tile, Seigaiha and Asanoha
  • Textures: Plain, Washi, Rice paper, Cotton, Kozo and more
  • Shelf counters (optional)
Notebook

A notebook for every book.

Every book has its own notebook, with two tabs. In Notes you write what you think of the book. In Context you keep a journal of what was happening in your life while you read it: where you were, what music you listened to, what else you were reading and how you felt.

Each book

Everything around each book.

A wishlist for the books you want to buy. Books are shared with a QR code and the library is exported as a backup.
