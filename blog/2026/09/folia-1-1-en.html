Folia 1.1

Version 1.1 "Céline"

Introductions are like the table of contents. What matters comes after.

Changelog — version 1.1 (2026-09-29)

New features

  • iPad support, with shelves, grids, and cards that use the whole screen instead of staying as narrow as on iPhone.
  • Rereading: "Read again" now lets you pick the date for the reread, and the book's detail page keeps a history of past rereads.
  • Annotate the book from the reading room: a new field in the progress panel to write something about the book itself, without having to go to the Notebook.
  • Customizable moods and vibes: moods and vibes are no longer a fixed list — you can create, rename, or delete your own.
  • Mural view: the whole library together in columns of covers of different sizes, with no shelves.
  • Large-cover view for the Wishlist, as an alternative to the usual list.
  • Page count on Wishlist and Capture cards.
  • Selectable typeface and font size for the Notebook, from Settings.
  • Sort and group the wishlist by year, genre, or page count.
  • Location of a captured book, with a small map and the option to open it in Apple Maps, edit it, or remove it.
  • Tag stamps, built from the same textures used for book spines.
  • Multi-column shelves on iPad: in the spine view, with enough width the library arranges itself into 2 or 3 parallel shelf columns instead of one stretched-out column. Off by default — turn it on from Settings.
  • Group the Library by genre or tag, in addition to the usual reading status. For now, spine view only.
  • Recording progress asks a bit more than before: beyond "Something about the book," it now also asks for the date, what else you were reading, and what music you were listening to — the same fields as a Context entry in the Notebook.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed: available tags not yet added to a book couldn't be deleted with a long press.
  • Fixed: a book's reason for being added to the Wishlist was lost when moving it to the Library.
  • Fixed: moving a book from Wishlist to Library left some buttons floating on screen with no purpose.
  • Fixed: in Capture and Wishlist, you couldn't scroll to fill in Genre or Author while the keyboard was open.
  • Fixed: iOS 26's new iPad tab bar covered each screen's own buttons.
  • Fixed: photos taken at a bookstore looked wrong when used as a cover, since they were never cropped.
  • Fixed: a genre typed in Capture could get lost before deciding wishlist or discard.
  • Fixed: a book already moved to the Library could reappear in Capture as if it were a new photo.
  • Fixed: recording progress from "Continue reading" saved nothing to the Diary — place, mood, and notes were lost, unlike the other two ways of recording progress.
  • Fixed: the screen's header peeked out from behind the floating card in Capture or Wishlist.
  • Fixed: square photos in the Library's cover view looked shrunken with a colored margin around them instead of filling the same size as generated covers.

Enjoy!
— L.D.
