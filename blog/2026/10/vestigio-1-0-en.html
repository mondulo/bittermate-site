Vestigio

I'm always coming across things on the street that I want to remember: a sign, a façade, a shop I want to visit, a detail I don't want to lose. I almost always end up with a photo lost in my camera roll among hundreds of others.

That's why I made Vestigio.

Vestigio is an app for saving what you find on the street: you take a photo, the app remembers where you were, and it stays anchored on the map.

Trace, footprint, place.

Vestigio also includes:

  • Geolocation, so every photo and note is tied to the place where you made it.
  • A proximity radar, to alert you when you pass near something you wanted to remember.
  • Expiration, for things that only matter for a while.
  • Four canvases to view your notes: Map, Board, Mural, and Calendar.
  • Universal capture, using the iOS share sheet to save to Vestigio whatever you find in any other app.
  • Material design, so your notes feel like objects rather than rows in a list.
  • Media organization, for photos and other resources attached to each note.
  • Local-first privacy: your data lives on your device.

It's a 1.0 and there's still room to improve. But it already does what I wanted: what catches my eye on the street doesn't get lost.

— L.D.
