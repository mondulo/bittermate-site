Bonus Track

Music player for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

App StoreMac
Playback

Every detail of the sound, under control.

Repeat modes, shuffle, gapless playback and crossfade. ReplayGain to even out volume, a sleep timer and a queue you can reorder by hand.

  • Control Center and lock screen
  • Widgets
  • Editable queue
Library

Five views for the same library.

List, Covers, Albums, Artists and Genres. MP3 import, metadata editing, artwork search, star ratings, batch actions and tools for duplicates.

Playlists

By hand or by rules.

Manual playlists with color, icon or emoji, and M3U import and export. Smart playlists are built with AND / OR rules.

  • Color, icon or emoji
  • Import and export M3U
  • AND / OR rules
Appearance

More than 20 themes, and your own.

Accessible contrast, custom themes, miniplayer styles, Now Playing XL, fonts and alternative app icons.

Mac

Also on Mac.

On the desktop

List and Now Playing, at desktop size.

The same library, with more room to view and edit.

Features

Everything it includes.

Library
  • MP3 import
  • Five views: List, Covers, Albums, Artists and Genres
  • Metadata editing
  • Artwork search and filling in missing covers
  • Use the cover of another song by the same artist
  • Star ratings
  • Batch actions
  • Duplicate finder
  • Real-time search
Playback
  • Repeat and shuffle, free or by deck
  • Gapless and crossfade
  • ReplayGain and volume normalization
  • Sleep timer by time or at the end of the song
  • Editable start and end of each song
  • Exact point at which a song counts as played
  • Up Next: queue with total duration, and adding from playlists or multiple selection
  • Soft stop when pausing
  • Keep the last volume level (optional)
  • Control Center, lock screen, Dynamic Island and headphones
Playlists
  • Color, icon, emoji and order of your choice
  • Shades of a secondary theme color or complementary colors
  • M3U import and export
  • Smart playlists with multiple AND / OR criteria and a limit
  • Criterion for songs that are in no playlist
  • Criteria import and export
  • When a smart playlist ends, the playlist is shown
  • Glass “Add to playlist” panel, with soft edges in each playlist’s color and a slider to adjust them
Themes and appearance
  • More than 20 themes, light and dark
  • Themes with two main colors: 138 combinations, plus your own
  • Theme creator with more than one main color
  • Redesigned theme gallery on iPad, with preview thumbnails
  • Redesigned Appearance panel, with better previews and more options
  • Accessible contrast
  • Fonts and title capitalization
  • Alternative icons, including Christmas ones and one for iPod nostalgics
  • Redesigned top bar on iPhone
Now Playing and miniplayer
  • Colors taken from the cover, with a selector: none, theme or cover
  • Outlined style for buttons
  • Now Playing XL, also with the cover only
  • Hideable bottom bar
  • Mode button on the corner of the cover, which indicates that it maximizes
  • Tapping the artist opens Artists in Library and highlights it; tapping the song opens the album
  • Gradient playback line, with a thickness equal to the square scrub and optional shadow or border
  • Miniplayer: swipe to change song, line styles, nano style and complementary colors
Widgets
  • Open any playlist from the lock screen or the app screen
  • Two giant Now Playing widgets
  • Playback widgets
iCloud and devices
  • Syncs playlists, smart playlists with their criteria, metadata and ratings, artwork, playlist icons and colors, and play counts
  • Music files are not uploaded
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac
  • On iPad: playlist sidebar, column view and an option to hide playlist names
Statistics and extras
  • Usage statistics and unlockable achievements
  • Total plays per device
  • “About”: a quote for each version, collectible record sleeves and a link to the changelog
  • Song count on import
  • Status banner when metadata and covers are completed
  • “More apps by BitterMate”
